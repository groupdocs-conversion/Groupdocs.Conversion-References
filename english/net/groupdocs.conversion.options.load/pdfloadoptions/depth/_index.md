## PdfLoadOptions.Depth property

Implements [`Depth`](../../../groupdocs.conversion.contracts/idocumentscontainerloadoptions/depth) How many levels of contained documents the conversion goes down, counted from the document the conversion starts from, which is level 0: 0 or a negative value converts no contained document, 1 converts the documents it contains directly, 2 also the documents those contain, and so on. On the document the conversion starts from, it is the limit for the whole conversion. Returned by the load options callback for a contained document at level L, it limits the documents that document contains to level L + Depth, never deeper than the limit of the document the conversion starts from. Default: 2
