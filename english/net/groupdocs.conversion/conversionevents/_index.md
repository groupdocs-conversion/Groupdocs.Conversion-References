## ConversionEvents class

Aggregates conversion lifecycle event handlers. Pass an instance to the [`Converter`](../converter) constructor's `events` parameter or to the fluent `WithEvents` method. Prefer this over the individual [`ConverterSettings`](../convertersettings) handler properties, which are obsolete.

For such an item the context carries its name, hierarchy level and item index, and [`Unknown`](../../groupdocs.conversion.filetypes/filetype/unknown) as its source and target format. Also fired once per contained item that cannot be converted because it is corrupted or password-protected. Such an item is delivered unchanged, as its original bytes, and the conversion of the remaining items continues; the context carries its name, its detected source format, hierarchy level and item index. When that failure occurs while a page of the item is saved in a page-by-page conversion, [`OnPageFailed`](./onpagefailed) is fired instead. It reports the final outcome of a document only: when a load fails and the load options delegate is asked again ([`Attempt`](../loadcontext/attempt) 2), it is fired after that attempt was declined or failed, and never for a document the second attempt loads. |
